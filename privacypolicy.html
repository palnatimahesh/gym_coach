Privacy Policy

Athletic Coach is a personal training app. It has no accounts, no servers and no analytics.

Where your data lives

Workouts, measurements, check-ins and settings are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded by the app. Automatic backups are saved as files on your device (Documents/AthleticCoach), and Android may also back the app up to your own Google account through its standard Auto Backup feature.

Health data

If you choose to connect Health Connect, the app reads these records, read-only: sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and steps. They are used only to calculate your daily readiness and are shown inside the app. The app never writes health data, never shares it with anyone, and never sends it off your device. You can disconnect at any time in the app or in Health Connect settings.

Third parties

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts when online. No other third-party services are used.

Deleting your data

Settings, Reset All Data erases everything stored by the app. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device.
